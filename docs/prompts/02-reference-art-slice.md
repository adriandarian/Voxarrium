# M2 — prove the art, not the city

Require M1 locomotion/capture evidence. Inspect actual reference PNGs and ART_DIRECTION.md. The master is city-scale art direction; the LEFT side of the Godot comparison is the rural detail target. Do not copy the Godot/Unreal/Blender shortcomings.

First match the slice's massing, terraces, house/bridge silhouettes, path hierarchy and playable dimensions in graybox. Obtain composition review before detailing. Unseen rear facades and street-level proportions are explicit design assumptions.

Then delegate at most two bounded tasks: (A) Blender hero cottage/bridge and export validation, (B) terrain/banks/foliage/material integration. Parent owns shared scale/palette and scene composition. Begin with one convincing cottage, not thirty generic modules. Use actual thick roofs, readable openings, varied crowns, irregular terrain transitions and purposeful close-range detail. No universal tiled ground or giant square cliff grids.

Validate Blender-to-runtime materials and directional-light response before instancing a library. Add clustered set dressing without blocking paths. Keep both open-world camera modes credible; no reference-plane projection, facade cheating or miniature-only framing.

Acceptance: same-seed eagle-eye + third-person + first-person captures, comparison report ordered by largest visual mismatches, memory/frame baseline, and explicit owner art approval. No 80–90% claims or arbitrary similarity scores. Do not advance to a whole city or hide art defects with weather/post-processing. Update STATUS.md and stop.
