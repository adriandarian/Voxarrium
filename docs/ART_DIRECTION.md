# Art direction

## Authority
`reference/city-master.png` is the primary city-composition and palette reference. `reference/experiments/godot-attempt.png` is a comparison image: LEFT is the rural target, RIGHT is an earlier Godot attempt. The Unreal and Blender images are earlier attempts, not approved final styles. Engine/model attribution is supplied by the owner, not inferred from pixels.

## Desired language
Warm varied terracotta and selective teal roofs; deep olive greens with sunlit moss; turquoise water with believable banks; cream plaster, weathered timber, aged stone; dense but navigable layered environments. Terraces and bridges create readable height relationships. Castle and towers provide orientation from streets.

Use large silhouettes, medium structural detail, and selective small detail together. Buildings need roof thickness, overhangs, dimensional openings, foundations and back/sides suitable for free camera movement. Ground requires organic transitions, not a universal square-grid surface. Masonry can be modular without every cliff becoming a perfect retaining-wall grid. Trees need varied branching/crowns and ground-level readability. Place clutter by function and ecology, not uniform noise.

## Avoid repeating earlier shortcomings
Godot comparison: sparse dressing and repetitive surfaces. Unreal attempt: cleaner lighting but conspicuous tile/grid language and simple crowns. Blender attempt: richer materials and vegetation, still diorama-like boundaries and repetitive tree forms. These are qualitative observations, not measured engine limitations.

## Review order
1. Composition, silhouettes and human scale.
2. Architecture, terrain and water-bank shapes.
3. Materials, light/value hierarchy and color.
4. Purposeful set dressing and vegetation diversity.
5. Animation and subtle finishing effects.

Density alone is not quality. Preserve clear paths and focal points. Do not solve a silhouette problem with more grass or hide geometry behind bloom, darkness, fog or aggressive depth of field.

## Three-view contract
Canonical eagle-eye frame; player-follow view; first-person entrance/bridge/cliff close-ups. No facade-only assets, projected reference planes, billboards at interaction distance, or inaccessible screenshot geometry. Distant impostors are allowed when measured and visually validated.

Save human art-review decisions in `reference/REVIEWS.md`. Do not assert an 80–90% match, invent metrics, or claim ground-level fidelity from an aerial capture.
