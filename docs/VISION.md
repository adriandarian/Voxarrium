# Vision

Walk into the city in `reference/city-master.png`: warm tiled roofs, ornate towers, turquoise waterways, multiple bridges, gardens, market streets and an elevated castle. The city should be inviting and legible at human scale, not just impressive from above.

## Player experience
Explore streets, cross bridges, climb terraces, talk to inhabitants, open usable doors, notice routines, weather, environmental motion and spatial sound. Default third-person; optional first-person using the same position, collision world, interactions and time. Eagle-eye exists for world overview and art review. A small optional map can come later.

The initial experience is peaceful exploration. Combat, economy, multiplayer, procedural quests, seasons, every-building interiors and model-generated dialogue are not committed features. No live model is required to play. Conversations begin as local authored content.

## Evidence gates
M0: local tools and reference provenance. M1: locomotion and camera-scale test with captures. M2: one carefully authored rural slice approved in all three camera modes. M3: add a few NPCs, day/night, wind, rain and audio to that approved slice. M4: representative district with modular buildings. M5: streamed multi-district city and scaling tests.

Match silhouettes, material hierarchy, composition and purposeful density before adding effects. Unseen sides, interiors, lane widths and exact dimensions are design decisions, not information recoverable with certainty from a single image.
